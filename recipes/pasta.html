Pasta with tomato sauce

The first recipe of the class

Added by: the class

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 1 can of tomatoes
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • a little salt

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water.
  2. Cook the tomatoes and the garlic for ten minutes.
  3. Mix the pasta and the sauce.
